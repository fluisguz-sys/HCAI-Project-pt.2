Internal Readiness Assessment

The Mirror Test

Before jumping in, social innovation requires looking inward. Evaluate your mindset across 4 vital dimensions to understand how you can best contribute to systemic change in Charlotte.

1

The Root Cause Check

Are you ready to shift focus from short-term charity (handing out blankets, temporary shelters) to fighting systemic drivers like exclusionary zoning, housing trust fund advocacy, and wage stagnation?

2

The Ego Check

Charlotte has established initiatives like Roof Above and the citywide A Home for All framework. Are you willing to align your energy with existing organizations rather than starting a brand new non-profit?

3

The Reality Check

High-density permanent supportive housing often faces intense NIMBY backlash in neighborhoods like Dilworth, Myers Park, or SouthPark. Can you stomach political friction and community pushback?

4

The Long-term Resilience Check

Housing First provides unconditional housing. Progress can be non-linear and human trauma is complex. Are you committed for the long haul, even when results take years?

Assessment Complete

Based on your responses, you are best suited for strategic systemic advocacy and data-driven partnership with Charlotte's existing networks!

How AI Multiplies Impact in Charlotte

AI isn't a replacement for housing—it is an intelligence layer that optimizes resource allocation, streamlines zoning reviews, and predicts risk before eviction occurs.

Predictive Eviction Prevention

Analyzing localized utility data, rent-to-income spikes, and emergency relief applications to deploy micro-grants *before* families enter the court system.

Zoning & Parcel Intelligence

Using computer vision and GIS mapping across Mecklenburg County to automatically identify underutilized land suitable for rapid supportive housing development.

Unified Shelter Coordination

Connecting real-time bed availability across Roof Above, Salvation Army Center of Hope, and regional providers to reduce wait times to zero.
